What will you send?

Your use case determines message type, pricing, opt-in rules, and technical setup. Pick the closest match — this shapes your entire launch plan.

Transactional notifications Most common
Order confirmations, delivery updates, OTPs, appointment reminders. One-way, triggered by events in your system.
Promotional campaigns Needs opt-in
Flash sales, loyalty offers, product launches. Rich media with image + button. Customers must explicitly opt in.
Two-way customer support Higher engagement
Live chat sessions, FAQ bots, agent handoff. Customer initiates — no opt-in required.
Mixed (transactional + promo) Full setup
Start with notifications to build trust, then unlock promotional messages after opt-in. Most scalable long-term.
